Suppose there are four processes P1, P2, P3, P4 and four resources R1, R2, R3, R4 in a system. The current allocation matrix and request matrix are as followed:

$$C=\begin{pmatrix}1&3&0&1\\4&1&2&2\\1&2&0&0\\2&0&0&1\end{pmatrix}$$

$$R=\begin{pmatrix}1&4&2&1\\0&2&3&3\\1&1&1&2\\3&1&1&1\end{pmatrix}$$

The availability resource vector is [3 2 2 1]. Determine whether the processes will be in deadlock or not.
