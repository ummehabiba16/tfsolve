Suppose in a particular environment with single processor, there are three types of processes: system processes, interactive processes and batch processes. Each system process has a predefined importance level which must be considered. All batch processes have known running time and are submitted at the same time. All interactive processes should be treated with equal importance. 50% of the total CPU time should be allocated to the system processes, 30% of the total CPU time should be allocated to the interactive processes and the rest of the total CPU time should be allocated to the batch processes. Now design a suitable scheduler for this environment. Make necessary assumptions and mention those assumptions.
