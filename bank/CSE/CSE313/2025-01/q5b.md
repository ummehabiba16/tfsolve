Formulate the equation for segment size in Log-structured File System (LFS).

$$D = \frac{F}{1-F} \times R_{peak} \times T_{position}$$

Suppose the disk spends 5ms time for positioning on an average and has 100MB/s of transfer rate. Calculate the segment size if we want effective rate to be 90%.
