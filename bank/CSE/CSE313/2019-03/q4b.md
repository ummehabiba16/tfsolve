Disk requests come in to the disk driver for cylinders 10, 22, 20, 2, 40, 6, and 38, in that order. A seek takes 6 ms per cylinder. How much seek time is needed for (i) First-come, first served, (ii) Shortest Seek First, (iii) Elevator algorithm (initially moving upward). In all cases, the arm is initially at cylinder 20.
