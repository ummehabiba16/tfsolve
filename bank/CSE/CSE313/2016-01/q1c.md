Suppose you are using paging technique for implementing virtual memory with 32 bit address space and 4 KB page size. You have decided to keep the entire page table for each process in main memory. As a result, you have to allocate a huge amount of memory for keeping the page table. Explain with necessary figures how you can solve this problem. You must prove the space efficiency of your technique.
