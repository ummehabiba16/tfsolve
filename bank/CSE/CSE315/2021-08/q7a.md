A UART sender X and receiver Y are both configured for 9 bit data, 1 start bit, 1 stop bit and odd-parity. Both X and Y are configured to the same baud rate. Suppose, receive buffer and shift register of Y are full and X sent a 9 bit character 1000 00101 to Y.

i. Draw the data framing diagram for the character 1000 00101.

ii. What will be the error(s) on the receiving side (FE/DOR/PE)? What is/are the reason(s) behind this?
