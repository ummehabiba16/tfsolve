You are given $W/\overline{R}$, $M/\overline{IO}$, and *HLDA* signals, where all these signals have their conventional meaning. Now, you need to generate four signals for DMA read and write operations. The four signals are –

i. WS (or signal to the source of a DMA write operation),

ii. WD (or signal to the destination of a DMA write operation),

iii. RS (or signal to the source of a DMA read operation), and

iv. RD (or signal to the destination of a DMA read operation).

Draw and elaborate a circuit that can generate the above signals.
