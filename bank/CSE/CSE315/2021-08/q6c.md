Suppose TIMER1 is running in CTC mode (WGM = 0100) where TOP = OCR1A. The value of OCR1A = 32768 and OCR1B = 7812. Pin OC1A and OC1B are configured to toggle on compare match. Initially OC1A and OC1B are both low and TCNT1 = 0. Prescaler is 0 i.e. TIMER1 clock is running as fast as internal 1MHz clock. Draw the wave shape of OC1A and OC1B with respect to TCNT1.
