You are given a microprocessor that has level-triggered $\overline{INTR}$ and *INTA* pins. Besides, you are given an external device that provides edge-triggered *INTR* signal.

You need to design a circuit that will ensure the available external edge-triggered *INTR* signal will work with the level-triggered $\overline{INTR}$ signal of the microprocessor.
