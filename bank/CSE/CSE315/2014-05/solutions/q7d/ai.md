Four applications of microcontrollers:

1. **Home appliances:** washing machines, microwave ovens, air conditioners, refrigerators (timing, sensing and motor control).
2. **Automobiles:** engine control units, ABS brakes, airbag controllers, dashboard instruments.
3. **Consumer electronics:** TV remote controls, MP3 players, digital cameras, toys.
4. **Measuring and medical instruments:** digital multimeters, thermometers, blood-pressure monitors.

(Also: robotics, electronic door locks and security systems, home automation, musical instruments, industrial control.)
