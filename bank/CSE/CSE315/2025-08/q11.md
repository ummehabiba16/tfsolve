Suppose you have three ATMega32 microcontrollers and some wires. Lets call these devices P, Q and R. P is a master device, the other two are slaves. Q and R devices each store an 8-bit number. We want to use SPI communication to send those numbers from the two slaves to the master P. The Master P will calculate the sum of these two numbers (in 8-bit) and send the sum to the two slaves. The overall transmission of data is sequentially mentioned below.

(i) Master P receives the 8-bit number from Q.

(ii) Master P receives the 8-bit number from R.

(iii) Master P calculates the 8-bit sum of the two received numbers and sends it to both Q and R.
