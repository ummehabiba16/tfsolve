Suppose you are trying out analog-to-digital conversion in ATMega32. You have set the value of the ADMUX register to `11001011`. The voltage readings at pins A0 to A3 are consecutively 4 V, 4.0064 V, 4.0128 V and 4.0192 V. You have connected an external capacitor to the AREF pin and a 5 V source to the AVCC pin. Determine the binary value stored in the ADCH and ADCL registers after the conversion is complete.
