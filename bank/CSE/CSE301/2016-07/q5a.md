Suppose that two teams are playing a series of games, each of which is independently won by team $A$ with probability $p$ and by team $B$ with probability $1-p$. The winner of the series is the first team to win $i$ games. Find the expected number of games that are played with (i) $i=2$, and (ii) $i=3$.
