A bus arrives at the 1st station with zero passengers on board. At the 1st station, 0, 1, or 2 passengers could get on the bus with probabilities 0.3, 0.5, 0.2, respectively. At every station, each passenger could get off the bus with a probability of 0.1. Find the expected value of the number of passengers that get off the bus at the 2nd station.
