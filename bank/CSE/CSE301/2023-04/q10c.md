Solve the following recurrence relation defined on the set of non-negative integers:

$$a_n=\begin{cases}1,&\text{if }n=0\\ \dfrac{4}{11},&\text{if }n=1\\ \dfrac{a_{n-1}a_{n-2}}{2a_{n-2}-a_{n-1}},&\text{otherwise}\end{cases}$$
