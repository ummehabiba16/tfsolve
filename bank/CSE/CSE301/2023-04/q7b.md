Derive Kolmogorov's forward and backward equations for a general continuous-time Markov chain using Chapman-Kolmogorov equations i.e. $P_{ij}(t+s)=\sum_{k=0}^{\infty}P_{ik}(t)P_{kj}(s)$. Now show that $\mathbf{P}(t)=\mathbf{P}(0)e^{\mathbf{R}t}$ where $\mathbf{P}(t)$ is the transition probability matrix and $\mathbf{R}$ is the instantaneous transition rate matrix.
