Let $m$ be an even positive integer. Assume that $\{a_1, a_2, \cdots, a_m\}$ and $\{b_1, b_2, \cdots, b_m\}$ are two complete sets of residue classes modulo $m$. Prove that the set $\{a_1+b_1, a_2+b_2, \cdots, a_m+b_m\}$ is not a complete residue class.
