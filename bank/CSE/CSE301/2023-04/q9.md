Consider a system of two servers where customers from outside the system arrive at server 1 at a Poisson rate 4 and at server 2 at a Poisson rate 5. The service rates of 1 and 2 are 8 and 10, respectively. A customer upon completion of service at server 1 is equally likely to go to server 2 or to leave the system; whereas a departure from server 2 will go 25 percent of the time to server 1 and will depart the system otherwise. Determine the limiting probabilities ($n$ customers at server 1 and $m$ customers at server 2), the average number of customers $L$, and the average amount of time spent by customers in the system $W$. It is known that the limiting probabilities of an M/M/1 queue is given by $\left(\frac{\lambda}{\mu}\right)^n\left(1-\frac{\lambda}{\mu}\right)$.
