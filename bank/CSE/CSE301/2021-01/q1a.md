Find integers $a$, $b$, and $c$ such that for all $m$, the following equation is satisfied.

$$m^3=a\binom{m}{3}+b\binom{m}{2}+c\binom{m}{1}$$

Also, find out the sum of the series $1^3+2^3+3^3+\ldots+n^3$ by using this equation.
