Consider the following bus ridership model:

- Suppose the bus is empty when it arrives at its first stop.
- Assume the bus is so large that it never becomes full, so the new passengers can always get on.
- At each stop, each passenger gets down from the bus, independently, with a probability of 0.2.
- Either 0, 1 or 2 new passengers get on the bus, with probabilities 0.5, 0.4 and 0.1, respectively. Passengers at successive stops are independent.
- Suppose the bus company charges Tk.15/- for each passenger who boards (gets on) at the first stop, but charges Tk.10/- for a passenger who joins at the second stop, regardless of the destination.

Find the expected total revenue from the first two stops.
