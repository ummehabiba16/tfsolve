A diagnostic clinic has two sequential service stages: preliminary screening (station A) and specialist consultation (station B). Patients arrive at the clinic according to a Poisson process with a rate of 2 patients per hour. A patient is admitted into the clinic only if station A is idle. Screening at station A takes an exponentially distributed time with a rate of 4 patients per hour. Upon completing the screening, the patient proceeds to station B for specialist consultation if it is idle; otherwise, they leave the clinic without seeing the specialist. The consultation time at station B is exponentially distributed with a rate of 2 patients per hour. Patients who complete the consultation then leave the clinic.

(i) Model the system as a continuous-time Markov process by defining appropriate system states.

(ii) Compute the steady-state probabilities.

(iii) What proportion of admitted patients complete the consultation with the specialist?

(iv) What is the average number of patients present in the clinic?

(v) What is the average amount of time an admitted patient spends in the clinic?
