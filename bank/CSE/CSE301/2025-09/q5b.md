Consider a two-chair shoe-shine shop, where customers arrive according to a Poisson process with rate $\lambda$ and enter the system only if chair 1 is free. Service times in chair 1 and chair 2 follow exponential distributions with rates $\mu_1$ and $\mu_2$, respectively. After completing service in chair 1, a customer proceeds to chair 2 if it is available; otherwise, they wait in chair 1. No customers enter directly into chair 2, and new arrivals are blocked when chair 1 is occupied.

(i) Construct an appropriate state space representation for the system.

(ii) Develop a complete state transition diagram.
