Consider a sequence of daily weather outcomes where each day is independently rainy with probability $p$ and sunny with probability $1-p$. A *run* is defined as a maximal sequence of consecutive days with identical weather. Apply the method of conditional expectation to compute the following:

(i) Expected length of the first run.

(ii) Expected length of the second run.
