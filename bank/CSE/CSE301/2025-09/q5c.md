A wildlife researcher wants to test whether a certain species of bird is equally likely to be spotted in each of five regions of a large forest. She conducts a survey and records the number of sightings in each region. The data are shown below.

| Region | Observed Sightings | Expected Sightings |
|:--|:-:|:-:|
| North | 18 | 20 |
| South | 21 | 20 |
| East | 19 | 20 |
| West | 22 | 20 |
| Central | 17 | 20 |

(i) At the 5% significance level, test whether the bird sightings are uniformly distributed across the regions using the chi-squared test. Show your calculations and clearly state your conclusion.

(ii) Suppose similar surveys were conducted in three additional forests, and the p-values from those tests were: 0.015, 0.04, and 0.22. Including the p-value you obtained in part (i), apply the Bonferroni method to determine which, if any, of the four tests remain significant.
