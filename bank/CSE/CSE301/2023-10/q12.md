You already know how to derive the number of tilings for a $2\times n$ rectangle using vertical and horizontal dominos. Now, an eccentric collector of $2\times n$ domino tilings pays 4 for each vertical domino and 1 for each horizontal domino. Calculate the number of tilings that are worth exactly $m$ under this criterion.
