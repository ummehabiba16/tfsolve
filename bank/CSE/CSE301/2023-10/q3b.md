A particle moves among $n+1$ vertices that are situated on a circle in the following manner. At each step it moves one step either in the clockwise direction with probability $p$ or the counterclockwise direction with probability $q=1-p$. Starting at a specified state, call it state 0, let $T$ be the time of the first return to state 0. Determine the probability that all states have been visited by time $T$.
