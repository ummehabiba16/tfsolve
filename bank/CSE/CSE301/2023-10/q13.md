Calculate the maximum number of regions defined by $n$ circles in a 2-dimensional space. You must systematically compute the closed form as a function of $n$ and provide necessary justifications.

Hints:

- How many regions are there when there are no circles drawn?
- How many regions are defined by one circle?
- Can you categorize them by bounded and unbounded regions?
- What happens to those regions when two circles intersect?
- Can the $n$-th circle intersect all previous $(n-1)$ circles?
- If so, how would it be possible?
