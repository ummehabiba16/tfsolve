A basketball player makes a shot with the following probabilities: 1/2 if he misses the last two times, 2/3 if he has hit one of his last two shots, 3/4 if he has hit both his last two shots. Formulate a Markov Chain to model his shooting, and compute the limiting fraction of the time he hits a shot.
