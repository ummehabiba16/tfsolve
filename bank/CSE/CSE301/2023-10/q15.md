Alice and Bob are sending cryptic messages to each other. Let $p$ and $q$ be distinct primes and $n=pq$ and $t=(p-1)(q-1)$. Let $e, d$ be positive integers such that $ed\equiv1\pmod t$. Alice takes a message, $M$ (an integer relatively prime to $n$), and sends $C=M^e\bmod n$ to Bob. Bob receives $C$ and computes $M'=C^d\bmod n$. Can you determine that whether the following statement is true or not: $M\equiv M'\pmod n$?
