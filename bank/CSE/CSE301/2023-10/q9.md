You are presented with a new variation of the Josephus problem: as usual, we begin with a group of $n$ individuals numbered from 1 to $n$ arranged in a circular formation. However, in this variant, we eliminate the first person of a consecutive pair until only one individual remains. For instance, when $n=10$, the sequence of eliminations is 1, 3, 5, 7, 9, 2, 6, 10, 8, resulting in the survival of individual 4. Formulate the recurrence relations for determining the survivor's number in this new variation of the Josephus problem. Remember to specify the base case too.

N.B.: You are not required to find the closed form.
