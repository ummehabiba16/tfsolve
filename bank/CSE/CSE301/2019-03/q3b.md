A particle moves among $n+1$ vertices that are situated on a circle in the following manner. At each step it moves to the next vertex either in the clockwise direction with probability $p$ or the counterclockwise direction with probability $q=1-p$. Starting at a specified vertex, call it vertex 0, let $T$ be the time of the first return to vertex 0. Find the probability that all vertices have been visited by time $T$.
