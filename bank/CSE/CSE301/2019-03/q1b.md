Two chips are drawn at random without replacement from a box that contains five chips numbered 1 through 5. If the sum of chips drawn is even, the random variable $X$ equals 5; if the sum of chips drawn is odd, then $X=-3$.

(i) Find the moment-generating function (mgf) for $X$.

(ii) Use the mgf to find the first and second moments.

(iii) Find the expected value and variance of $X$.
