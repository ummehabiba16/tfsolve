Consider a Poisson process with rate $\lambda$, and let us denote the time of the first event by $T_1$. Further, for $n>1$, let $T_n$ denote the elapsed time between the $(n-1)$st and the $n$th event. The sequence $\{T_n, n=1,2,\ldots\}$ is called the sequence of inter-arrival times. Show that $T_n$, $n=1,2,\ldots$, are independent identically distributed exponential random variables having mean $1/\lambda$.
