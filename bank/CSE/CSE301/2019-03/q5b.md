What is spectrum ($\mathrm{Spec}(\alpha)$) of a real number? Let $n$ be an arbitrary positive integer. Let $p$ be the number of elements of $\mathrm{Spec}(\sqrt2)$ that are less than or equal to $n$. Let $q$ be the number of elements of $\mathrm{Spec}(2+\sqrt2)$ that are less than or equal to $n$. Prove that $p+q=n$.
