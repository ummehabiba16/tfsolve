A certain word is equally likely to be in any one of three different files. Let, $p_i$ be the probability that you will find the word upon making a quick examination of file $i$, if the word is, in fact, in file $i$, for $i=1,2,3$, Given $p_1=1/2$, $p_2=1/3$, $p_3=1/4$. Suppose you look into file 2 and do not find the word. What is the probability that the word is in file 2?
