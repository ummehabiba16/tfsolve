In an election, candidate $A$ receives $n$ votes, and candidate $B$ receives $m$ votes where $n > m$. Assuming that all orderings are equally likely, demonstrate that the probability that $A$ is always ahead of $B$ in the count of votes is $(n-m)/(n+m)$.
