A taxi driver provides service in two zones of a city. Fares picked up in zone $A$ will have destinations in zone $A$ with probability 0.6 or in zone $B$ with probability 0.4. Fares picked up in zone $B$ will have destinations in zone $A$ with probability 0.3 or in zone $B$ with probability 0.7. The driver's expected profit for a trip entirely in zone $A$ is 6; for a trip entirely in zone $B$ is 8; and for a trip that involves both zones in 12. Find the taxi driver's average profit per trip.
