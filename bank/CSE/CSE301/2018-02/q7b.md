Consider a gambler who at each play of the game has probability $p$ of winning one unit and probability $q=1-p$ of losing one unit. Assuming that successive plays of the game are independent, what is the probability that, starting with $i$ units, the gambler's fortune will reach 0 before reaching $N$?
