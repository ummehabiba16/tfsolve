We have an ant and a worm on the tip of two different horizontal rubber strings. Both strings are of length 100 cm. The ant moves 1 cm per second and the worm moves 2 cm per second. The rubber string of the ant is stretched 100cm each second. On the other hand, the rubber string of the worm is stretched 100 cm each 0.5 second. During the stretching operation the ant and the worm maintain their relative positions on their respective strings.

Prove or disprove that, after $n$ seconds (where $n\ge1$ and $n\in\mathbb{N}$),

*(The fraction travelled by worm -- the fraction travelled by ant)* $<2\%$
