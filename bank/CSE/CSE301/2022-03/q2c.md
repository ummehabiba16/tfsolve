Let $N$ be a Poisson random variable with mean $\lambda$. Given $N$, let $X$ be another binomial random variable with parameters $N$ and $p$ ($p$ being the probability).

Let $Y=N-X$ be a random variable and given that $Y$ is non-negative. Prove that $Y$ is a Poisson random variable with mean $\lambda(1-p)$.
