A miner is trapped in a mine (M1) containing three doors. The first door (D1) leads to a tunnel that takes him to safety after 4 hours of travel. The second door (D2) leads to a tunnel that returns him to the mine after 2 hours of travel. However, the third door (D3) leads to another mine (M2) after 3 hours. Now, the second mine (M2) has two other doors. First one (D4) leads to safety after 1 hour of travel. But the second door (D5) leads to a tunnel that returns him to the same mine (M2) after 2 hours of travel. Assume that the miner is at all times equally likely to choose any one of the doors (in M1, the miner can choose between D1, D2, D3, and in M2, the miner can choose between D3, D4, D5). What is the expected duration of time until the miner reaches safety?
