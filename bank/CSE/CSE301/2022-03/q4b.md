Consider a shoe shop consisting of two chairs. Suppose that an entering customer first will go to chair 1. When his work is completed in chair 1, he will go either to chair 2 if that chair is empty or else wait in chair 1 until chair 2 becomes empty. Suppose that a potential customer will enter this shop as long as chair 1 is empty. (Thus, for instance, a potential customer might enter even if there is a customer in chair 2.) Suppose that potential customers arrive in accordance with a Poisson process at rate $\lambda$, and that the service times for the two chairs are independent and have respective exponential rates of $\mu_1$ and $\mu_2$.

(i) Draw a state diagram of the system and write down balance equations for each state.

(ii) What proportion of potential customers enters the system?

(iii) What is the mean number of customers in the system?

(iv) What is the average amount of time that an entering customer spends in the system?

(v) What proportion of entering customers are blockers?
