What is a PEAS description? Suppose you are playing FIFA 2011 (Football Game) on your PC. There are 22 agents. At any time, you can control exactly one of the 11 players of your team. And, all other 21 players are controlled by the PC according to some rules. Both teams optimize their actions with a goal to win the match. For this Task Environment describe its Characteristics (i.e. whether Fully-observable/Partially-observable, deterministic/stochastic, episodic/ strategic/sequential, static/dynamic, discrete/continuous, single agent/ multi-agent, competitive/cooperative).
