Suppose you are playing Crysis 2 on PC. You are controlling the protagonist (main character of the game). There are some hostile agents (hostile to you) searching for you. You have taken HIT-AND-RUN strategy, which means you shoot some hostile agent with your gun, then become invisible (yes you have that power!), and change places in the stealth mode (others cannot notice). Suggest some heuristics for the hostile agents as to how to search for you. It is given that, when you attack someone, everyone can hear the gun-shot and knows the location of the sound's source.
