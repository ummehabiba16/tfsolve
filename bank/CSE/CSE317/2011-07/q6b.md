What is MATERIAL-VALUE? Suppose you and your friend are playing Ludo game (2 players). Both of you have 4 pieces (i.e., "guti"). Design a scheme to assign a MATERIAL-VALUE to each of the pieces. You should provide a weight of each piece based on its maturity (i.e., whether it is "kacha guti" or "paka guti"). You should also consider safety of pieces (i.e., safe cells).
