The accuracy of an opinion poll is 98.5%. The poll is used to determine the popularity of a certain candidate X for the national election. Collected data shows that 3 in 5 people are in favor of the candidate. Find the followings--

(i) probability that the candidate is elected given the opinion poll is against the candidate.

(ii) probability that the candidate is defeated given the opinion poll is in favor of the candidate.
