**Intelligence.** The capacity to acquire and apply knowledge so as to perform well in an environment: to perceive, reason, learn and solve new problems, adapting behaviour to achieve goals. A quantifiable version: the ability to act so as to do well with respect to a performance measure, especially in situations not seen before.

**Artificial intelligence.** The field that studies and builds intelligent entities in machines: computational agents that perceive their environment and take actions that maximise their chances of success (acting rationally). It covers perception, reasoning, planning and decision-making, learning, and acting. Equivalently: the study and construction of agent programs that perform well in a given environment for a given agent architecture.

**Agent.** Anything that can be viewed as perceiving its environment through **sensors** and acting upon that environment through **actuators**, e.g. a human, a robot, a thermostat, a software bot. Its behaviour is described by the agent function mapping percept sequences to actions.
