Give a complete problem formulation for each of the following. Choose a formulation that is precise enough to be implemented.

(i) Using only four colors, you have to color a planar map in such a way that no two adjacent regions have the same color.

(ii) A 3-foot-tall monkey is in a room where some bananas are suspended from the 8-foot ceiling. the monkey would like to get the bananas. the room contains two stackable, movable, and climbable 3-foot-high crates.
