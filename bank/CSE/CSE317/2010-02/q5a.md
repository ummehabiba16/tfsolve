Convert the following English statements into first order logic statements: (10+10=20)

(i) Every boy or girl is a child.

(ii) Every child gets a doll or a train or a bi-cycle.

(iii) No boy gets a doll.

(iv) No child who is good gets bi-cycle.

(v) Jack is a boy.

Using above five axioms, construct a proof by resolution of the following statement:

"If Jack does not get a train, than Jack is not a good boy."
