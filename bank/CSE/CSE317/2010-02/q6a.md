1% of woman over age forty who are screened have breast cancer. 80% of woman, who really do have breast cancer will have a positive mammography, (meaning test is positive). 9.6% of woman who don't actually have breast cancer will also have a positive mammography. Now, answer the following questions:

(i) If a woman in this age group gets a positive mammography, what is the probability that, she actually has the breast cancer? (10)

(ii) Say, a woman gets a positive mammography test M1, goes back and gets a second mammography M2, which is also positive. Use the naive Bayes assumption to calculate the probability that she has breast cancer given the results from these two tests. (10)
