Soundness of an inference rule (procedure) i is defined as:

Whenever $KB \vdash_i \alpha$ for any sentence $\alpha$, it is also true that $KB \models \alpha$. Consider the following inference rule in propositional logic where J, H and P are proposition symbols. Prove whether or not it is a sound rule of inference.

$$\frac{(J \land H) \Rightarrow P, \quad \neg P}{\neg J \lor \neg H}$$
