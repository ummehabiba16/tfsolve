Consider the following cell phone domain. Having a charged battery (CB) enables a cell phone to be operational (OP). Having an operational cell phone, being in the coverage area (CA), and knowing the number to call (KN) enables the placing of a call (PC). Assume there are no other dependencies. All random variables are Boolean. Draw a Bayesian Network for this domain, including nodes, arcs and the form of each conditional probability table (CPT) at each node.
