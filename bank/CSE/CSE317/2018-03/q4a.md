A conditional probability distribution $P(X \mid e)$ can be expressed as $\alpha P(X,e)$. What is $\alpha$ in the above expression? If you have a third variable y, and you are given a joint distribution of $P(X,e,y)$, how do you express $P(X \mid e)$? (5+5)
