Consider a scenario, where 5% of the populations have some sort of cancer (cr) diseases. Among the cancerous population, 40% of them have chronic cough (cc) as one of their symptoms. There are many other reasons for chronic cough, and among all chromic cough patients, 9 out 10 people are non-cancerous. If you have a symptom of chronic cough, what will be the probability that you will be diagnosed with cancer?
