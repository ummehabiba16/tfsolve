Sometimes Markov decision processes (MDPs) are formulated with a reward function $R(s, a)$ that depends on the action $a$ taken on state $s$ or with a reward function $R(s, a, s')$ that also depends on the outcome state $s'$. Express the Bellman equations for these formulations.
