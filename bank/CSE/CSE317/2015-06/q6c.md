After a yearly checkup, your friends is informed by his doctor that he has been tested positive for a very serious disease. The accuracy of the test is 99% (i.e. the probability of testing positive when indeed on has the disease is 0.99, as is the probability of testing negative when one does not have the disease). The disease is also very rare, only 1 out of 100,000 people gets affected by it. Now, given the above scenario, find out the actual probability of your friend having (and not having) the disease.
