Given the following joint probability distribution $P(X,Y)$, compute the following conditional probabilities: i) $P(+x \mid -y)$, ii) $P(-y \mid +x)$, iii) $P(+x \mid +y)$, and iv) $P(-x \mid +y)$.

| X | Y | P |
|:-:|:-:|:-:|
| +x | +y | 0.2 |
| +x | -y | 0.3 |
| -x | +y | 0.4 |
| -x | -y | 0.1 |
