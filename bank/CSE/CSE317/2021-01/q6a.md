Consider a dataset with two Boolean attributes A and B. The dataset consists of 185 examples labeled with + and - outputs.

| Example | Count |
|:--|:-:|
| < (A=0, B=0), - > | 50 examples |
| < (A=0, B=1), - > | 25 examples |
| < (A=1, B=0), - > | 10 examples |
| < (A=1, B=1), + > | 100 examples |

Based on the information gain concept, draw the decision tree. Also show the calculation of information gain at each step of the tree construction.
