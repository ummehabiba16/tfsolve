The missionaries and cannibals problem is usually stated as follows. Three missionaries and three cannibals are on one side of a river, along with a boat that can hold one or two people. Find a way to get everyone to the other side without ever leaving a group of missionaries in one place outnumbered by the cannibals in that place.

i. Formulate the problem precisely, making only those distinctions necessary to ensure a valid solution. Draw a diagram of the complete state space.

ii. Describe an appropriate algorithm to solve the problem optimally. Is it a good idea to check for repeated states?

iii. Why do you think people have a hard time in solving this puzzle, given that the state space is so simple?
