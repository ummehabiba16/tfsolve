A CSP has a set $X = \{x_1, \ldots, x_n\}$ of variables, each having a domain $D = \{d_1, \ldots, d_n\}$ of values. In addition, the CSP has a set $C = \{C_1, \ldots, C_m\}$ of constraints, each relating to a subset of $X$ and specifying the allowable combinations of assignments to the variables in that subset.
