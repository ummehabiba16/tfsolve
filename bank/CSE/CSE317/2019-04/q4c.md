Consider a job shop scheduling problem where you have two jobs, each of the form [AddEngine, Addwheels, Inspect]. For the first job J1, the durations of three activities are 30, 30, and 10, respectively. Also, for the second job J2, the durations of three activities are 60, 15, and 10, respectively. Assume, you have one engine hoist, one wheel station, two inspectors, and 500 nuts. Find out the makespan by minimum slack algorithm. What is the makespan, if you have two engine hoists, one wheel station, one inspector, 500 nuts with the duration of the activities of second job J2 being 40, 15, and 10, respectively.
