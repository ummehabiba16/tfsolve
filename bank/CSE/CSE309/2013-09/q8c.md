Explain that any SDD having a production $A \to B\ C$ with semantic rules $A.s = B.b$ and $B.i = f(C.c, A.s)$ can be neither S-attributed nor L-attributed SDD. Here, $s$ is a synthesized attribute and $i$ is an inherited attribute while $b$ and $c$ can be of any type.
