Below is a grammar for variable declarations of the forms such as `int i, k[2], j[2][3];` Now write an SDT to save the type and width information of all the declared identifiers in the symbol table. Assume that (i) types are represented as type expressions and (ii) widths of an integer and a float are 4 and 8 bytes, respectively. Make other justified assumptions as required.

$$V \to T\ D\ \textbf{;}$$

$$T \to \textbf{int} \mid \textbf{float}$$

$$D \to D_1\ \textbf{,}\ \textbf{id}\ C \mid \textbf{id}\ C$$

$$C \to \textbf{[}\ \textbf{num}\ \textbf{]}\ C_1 \mid \epsilon$$
