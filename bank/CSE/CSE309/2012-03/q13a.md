Consider the following grammar for declaration statement.

$$D \to T\ \textbf{id};$$

$$T \to B\ C$$

$$B \to \textbf{int} \mid \textbf{float}$$

$$C \to P\ A$$

$$P \to *\ P \mid \epsilon$$

$$A \to [\textbf{num}]\ A \mid \epsilon$$

(i) Construct parse trees for the following declaration statements according to the above grammar.

(1) `int x;`

(2) `float * y;`

(3) `int * [10] z;`

(ii) Design and SDD for the above grammar that computes the type and width of a declared identifier. Assume, the width of an integer is 4 and that of a float is 8.
