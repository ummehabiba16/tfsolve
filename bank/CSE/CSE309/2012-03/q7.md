Eliminate left recursion from the grammar,

$$A \to B \mid a \mid CBD$$

$$B \to C \mid b$$

$$C \to A \mid c$$

$$D \to d$$

If you encounter a production like $X \to X$ at some point of elimination, you can drop it since it does not affect the language.
